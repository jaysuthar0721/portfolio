PID Motor Control with Simulink

A control-system study using Simulink PID blocks to examine roll response, motor mixing, and the effect of changing vehicle geometry.

Controls approach

The study used built-in Simulink PID blocks in a drone-control context, beginning with a single-axis roll model and extending toward motor mixing and moving-arm effects. The purpose was to understand controller response before relying on hardware-only tuning.

This work overlaps with the Variable Geometry Drone project, where the final hardware controller used proportional and derivative control after the integral term proved too aggressive during test-stand tuning.
