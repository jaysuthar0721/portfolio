Jay Suthar

[ Colorado State University / Class of 2027 ]

I’m a mechanical engineering student at Colorado State University, focused on aerospace systems, propulsion, and fluid dynamics. Through Ram Rocketry, research, and course projects, I work across mechanical design, simulation, electronics, and testing.

For a variable geometry drone, I built the wireless control system, integrated the motion sensor, and tuned the attitude controller on a test stand. This portfolio covers the decisions behind that work, what happened during testing, and what I learned along the way.
