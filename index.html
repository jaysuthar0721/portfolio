Mechanical engineering / Aerospace systems

Jay Suthar

[ Colorado State University / Class of 2027 ]

I am a mechanical engineering student at Colorado State University who studies aerospace systems, propulsion and experimental engineering. It is my preference to manage problems - applying basic physics laws during the processes of design, simulation, fabrication plus testing. There is a specific focus in my work on fluid dynamics and systems that operate at high speeds or under extreme conditions.

By participating in research, Ram Rocketry but also practical engineering projects, I have gained experience in mechanical design, computational fluid dynamics, propulsion, control systems and experimental testing. The portfolio contains documentation of the projects, systems as well as lessons that influence my engineering methods.
